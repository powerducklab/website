1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

Cookies may be set by the website you are visiting ("first-party cookies") or by third-party services embedded on the page ("third-party cookies"). First-party cookies are set by and belong to the website you are visiting. Third-party cookies are set by organizations other than the website you are visiting, such as analytics providers, advertising networks, and social media platforms.

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies: these are temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser. They are used to remember your actions during a single browsing session.
  • Persistent cookies: these remain on your device for a set period of time or until you manually delete them. They are used to remember your preferences and settings across multiple browsing sessions.

We also use similar technologies such as local storage, session storage, pixel tags, and web beacons. These technologies are covered by the same principles described in this notice.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use cookies for the following purposes:

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core features such as navigation, security, and accessibility. Without these cookies, the website may not work as intended and you may not be able to use certain features.

Essential cookies are always active and cannot be disabled in our cookie consent tool, as they are required for the basic functionality of the website. You may be able to block them through your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of the website.

2.2 Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your choices and preferences, such as your selected theme (light or dark), language, region, and font size. They make your experience more personalized and convenient by remembering your settings across multiple visits.

Preference cookies are optional and require your consent. You can enable or disable them at any time through our cookie consent tool or your browser settings.

2.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect anonymous information about how visitors use our website, such as which pages are visited most often, how long visitors stay on each page, which pages they came from, and any errors encountered. This information helps us improve the website's performance, user experience, and content.

We use privacy-friendly analytics that do not track individual users across websites and do not collect personally identifiable information. The data collected is aggregated and anonymized.

Analytics cookies are optional and require your consent. You can enable or disable them at any time through our cookie consent tool or your browser settings.

2.4 Marketing Cookies

We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies on our website. We do not serve targeted advertisements, and we do not share your browsing behavior with advertising networks or third parties for marketing purposes.

If we decide to use marketing cookies in the future, we will update this notice and obtain your consent before setting any marketing cookies.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Our website may embed content from third-party services, which may set their own cookies. We do not control these third-party cookies, and they are not covered by this notice. Please refer to the respective third-party privacy policies for more information about their cookie practices.

The third-party services we use may include:

  • Paddle: our payment processor, which may set cookies for billing and fraud prevention. See Paddle's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub: embedded code snippets and links to our open-source repositories. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Font providers: web fonts from third-party font services. See the respective provider's privacy policy.

We only embed third-party content that is necessary for the functionality of our website. We do not embed third-party advertising or tracking services.

5. Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we use the following similar technologies:

  • Local Storage: allows us to store data on your device, such as your theme preference and settings. Data stored in local storage persists across browser sessions until you manually clear it.
  • Session Storage: allows us to store data temporarily during your browsing session. Data stored in session storage is deleted when you close your browser.
  • Pixel Tags / Web Beacons: small, transparent images that allow us to track whether you have visited a particular page or interacted with certain content. We use these for internal analytics only.
  • ETags: HTTP headers that allow us to cache content and track usage patterns. ETags are used for performance optimization and analytics.

These technologies are subject to the same principles as cookies described in this notice. You can control local storage and session storage through your browser settings.

6. How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

6.1 Cookie Consent Tool

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of the website.

6.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View and delete individual cookies.
  • Block all cookies.
  • Allow cookies only from trusted websites.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of our website and may prevent you from using certain features. Essential cookies cannot be disabled through our consent tool, but you may be able to block them through your browser settings.

6.3 Browser-Specific Instructions

For more information about how to control cookies in your browser, please refer to the following resources:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6.4 Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that allows you to signal your preference not to be tracked. Our website does not currently respond to DNT signals, as there is no universally accepted standard for how to respond to such signals. However, we respect your privacy and provide you with the ability to control cookies through our consent tool and browser settings.

7. Cookie Consent

In accordance with the ePrivacy Directive and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we require your consent before setting non-essential cookies on your device. Essential cookies are set without consent as they are necessary for the basic functionality of the website.

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept all: accept all cookies, including non-essential cookies.
  • Reject all: reject all non-essential cookies. Only essential cookies will be set.
  • Customize: choose which categories of non-essential cookies you want to accept.

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of the website. Your consent preferences will be stored in the "powerduck-cookie-consent" cookie for one year.

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing your consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

8. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time to reflect changes in our cookie practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated notice on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may also display a prominent notice on our website or send you an email notification.

We encourage you to review this Cookie Notice periodically to stay informed about our cookie practices. Your continued use of the website after the effective date of the updated notice constitutes your acceptance of the changes.
