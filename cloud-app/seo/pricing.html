What counts as a task?

Each task uses at most 1 count, including tools and batches. A valid result counts even if later work is cancelled or fails. No valid result, cache-only results and local checks are free.

When allowances reset

Free resets on the first day of each calendar month at 00:00 UTC. Resets each monthly billing period. Unused tasks do not roll over.

What stays local

Browsing files, Git/AST analysis and reading saved results do not consume AI tasks. Requested AI work sends selected context to managed model providers.

Plans & Billing

Existing credit subscriptions may retain their original billing rules. Your account and checkout show the applicable entitlement. Prices are in USD. Checkout confirms the final total and taxes.
